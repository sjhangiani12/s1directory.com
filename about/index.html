About

An S-1 is the document a company files with the SEC before it goes public. It is the most honest thing most companies ever write about themselves: what they do, how they make money, and what could go wrong.

S1 Directory collects 258 of them, from the companies that built the commercial internet. Each one is bound as a book, with a cover made from a public-domain artwork chosen for its story.

Filings link to the original documents on SEC EDGAR. IPO dates and offer prices come from the Jay Ritter IPO dataset and sec-api.io. Cover art comes from the Art Institute of Chicago open access collection (CC0). The company essays are short summaries. If you spot an error, open an issue.
