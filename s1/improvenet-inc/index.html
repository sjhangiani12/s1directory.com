Finding a Good Contractor

Priced March 15, 2000

Finding a reliable contractor has always been a gamble based on word of mouth. ImproveNet, founded in 1996, tried to make it easier by building an online service that screened contractors and matched them with homeowners planning a project. Contractors paid for the leads. It went public in March 2000, one of many companies of the time promising to bring an old local trade onto the web.

The Original Bet

ImproveNet bet that homeowners would trust a website to vet contractors, and that contractors would pay for qualified leads. It checked licenses and references and offered tools to estimate project costs. The idea was sound, and later companies built large businesses on it. In 2000, though, too few homeowners were looking for contractors online. Its service covered projects from kitchen remodels to new roofs.

A Remodel of Its Own

ImproveNet priced its IPO at $16 a share in March 2000 and listed on Nasdaq. Its shares fell steeply within months, and the company cut costs to survive. In 2002 it merged with eTechLogix, a private software firm based in Arizona, and new management took over. The combined company moved away from its original consumer focus. Its original idea was later proven by other home services sites.
