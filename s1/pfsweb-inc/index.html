The Dock Behind the Store

Priced December 02, 1999

Selling online is the easy part to see. Behind it sit warehouses, pick lists, shipping labels, returns and call centers. PFSweb, spun out of the computer supply distributor Daisytek, offered to run all of that for brands that wanted to sell directly on the web. When it filed to go public in late 1999, retailers and manufacturers were rushing online without knowing how they would deliver.

The Original Bet

The bet was that many companies would want to sell online but would not want to build their own fulfillment operations. PFSweb took over the work after the click: processing orders, storing goods, packing and shipping, handling payments, and answering customer calls. It brought the logistics experience of its parent to brands that had never shipped single orders to consumers.

Two Decades of Fulfillment

PFSweb priced at $17 a share in December 1999. Its shares fell with the market, but the business it chose proved durable as e-commerce kept growing. It served brands across fashion, health and consumer goods, and for a time it also ran an e-commerce agency. In 2023 the logistics company GXO acquired PFSweb, adding its fulfillment network to GXO's own.
