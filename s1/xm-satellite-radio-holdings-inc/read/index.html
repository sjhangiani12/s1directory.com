Prospectus Summary

This summary contains basic information about us and this offering. Because it is a summary, it does not contain all the information you should consider before investing in our common stock. Please read the entire prospectus carefully, including the section entitled "Risk Factors" and our financial statements and the related notes to those statements. This prospectus contains certain statistical data about our industry and the markets in which we plan to operate that comes from information published by or made available by consultants. Although we believe that this data is generally reliable, there is no assurance as to its accuracy. We caution you not to place undue reliance on this data. All numbers in this prospectus will be adjusted to reflect a for 1 stock split which will occur upon completion of this offering. Unless stated otherwise, all information in this prospectus assumes conversion of our outstanding convertible notes into common and preferred stock and no exercise of the underwriters' over-allotment option.

Our Business

We seek to become a premier nationwide provider of digital quality audio entertainment and information programming. We will transmit our service, which we will call "XM Radio," from our satellites to vehicle, home and portable radios. XM Radio plans to offer up to 100 channels of music, news, talk, sports and children's programming developed by us or third parties for a monthly subscription price of $9.95. We believe XM Radio will appeal to consumers because of our digital sound quality, variety of programming and nationwide coverage. We will build a subscriber base for XM Radio through multiple distribution channels, including an exclusive distribution arrangement with General Motors, other automotive manufacturers, car audio dealers and national electronics retailers. We expect to commence full commercial operation of our service in the second quarter of 2001.

We hold one of only two licenses issued by the Federal Communications Commission to provide satellite digital audio radio service in the United States. We will broadcast XM Radio throughout the continental United States from two of the most powerful commercial satellites available. These satellites, and a ground spare, are being built by Hughes Space and Communications Inc., the world's leading commercial satellite contractor, and Alcatel Space Industries, a leading manufacturer of satellite communications electronics. A network of terrestrial repeaters serving up to 70 of the largest metropolitan areas will augment our satellite signal coverage.

We have contracts with four leading consumer electronics manufacturers to develop, manufacture and distribute XM radios. Our radios will be capable of receiving both XM Radio and traditional AM/FM stations. One of these manufacturers, Delco Electronics Corporation, a subsidiary of Delphi Automotive Systems, is the leading original equipment manufacturer of radios for the automobile industry, producing approximately 33% of original equipment automobile radios in the United States in 1997. Delphi-Delco is also the leading manufacturer of automobile radios sold in General Motors vehicles. Two of our other manufacturers, Pioneer Electronics Corporation and Alpine Electronics, together produced over 31% of radios sold in the United States in 1997 for installation in automobiles after purchase, so-called aftermarket radios. SHARP Corporation, a consumer electronics company, will develop and distribute XM radios for home and portable use. STMicroelectronics, a leading car audio chipset manufacturer, will design and produce chipsets for XM radios. STMicroelectronics developed similar technology being used in an international satellite radio system.

We intend to offer our consumers a unique listening experience by providing up to 100 channels, coast-to-coast coverage and digital quality sound. We believe that the quality and diversity of our programming will be key drivers of consumer interest in our service. To that end, we have developed a unique programming strategy that offers consumers . Original music and talk channels created by the company . Channels created by brand name content providers . The availability of commercial-free and limited-advertising channels XM Originals Through a programming unit in the company called "XM Originals", we will create a significant number of original channel formats with content focusing on popular music such as oldies, rock and country, and on new and innovative formats, including jazz, blues, reggae and pop classical. These formats will include artists with strong music sales and concert revenue who do not get significant airplay on traditional AM/FM radio stations. We also intend to brand individual channels creating a specific station personality and image for each using compelling on-air talent and other techniques to attract listeners in our target market segments. We have hired a team of programming professionals with a proven track record of introducing new radio formats and building local and national listenership.

Our Investors and Strategic Partners We have raised $330.8 million in financing, net of expenses and repayment of debt, from investors and strategic partners. Our strategic investor group includes leaders in industries that are closely related to our business: automotive manufacturing, direct satellite broadcasting and radio broadcasting. Our Opportunity

We believe that there is a significant market for XM Radio. Market data show strong demand for radio service. Over 75% of the entire United States population age 12 and older listens to the radio daily, and over 95% listens to the radio weekly. People listen to the radio an average of 3.2 hours a day, with an estimated 42% of radio listening done in the car. In 1997, United States consumers bought approximately 15.7 million factory/dealer original equipment automobile radios, 7.4 million aftermarket automobile radios and 1.2 million aftermarket automobile CD changers. In addition, radio advertising revenue was $15.4 billion in 1998 and is projected to continue to grow at over 9% annually through 2002.

We believe our national subscription service will complement traditional local radio and that XM Radio will be attractive to underserved radio listeners who want expanded radio choices. Approximately 50% of all commercial radio stations in the top 268 radio markets in the United States use one of only three programming formats. Over 36 million people live beyond these 268 radio markets, limiting the variety and quality of their radio programming choices. In addition, artists representing 21% of music sales receive little or no airplay on traditional AM/FM radio.

We believe that people will subscribe to our service because we will offer a large number of channels, coast-to-coast coverage, diverse programming and superior digital quality sound. Consumers in the United States have demonstrated the willingness to pay for additional programming choices. As of year-end 1998, more than 76% of households in the United States subscribed to cable or satellite television services at an average cost of $31 per month. A market study conducted for us by Critical Mass Media, a leading radio research firm, projects that between 34 million and 43 million customers would be willing to pay approximately $9.95 per month for satellite radio service, and between $200 and $400 for a satellite radio.

Our Strategy

Offer Unique Programming Tailored for Specific Audiences We expect the diversity of our programming will appeal to a large audience, including urban and rural listeners of virtually all ages, ethnicities, economic groups and specialty interests. We intend to tailor our programming and marketing to appeal to specific groups that our research has shown are most likely to subscribe to our satellite radio service.

Establish Broad Distribution Channels for XM Radios We expect to establish several broad distribution channels for XM Radio including . Exclusive distribution agreement with General Motors. General Motors has agreed to exclusively distribute and market the XM Radio service and to install XM radios in General Motors vehicles. We are currently in discussions with other car manufacturers regarding additional distribution agreements.

. Distribution through radio manufacturers. We have an agreement with Delphi-Delco to develop, manufacture and distribute XM radios for installation in new cars, and have contracts with Pioneer, Alpine and SHARP, leading radio manufacturers with strong retail and dealer distribution networks in the United States.

. Rural market distribution/alternative distribution. We intend to market our satellite radio service in rural areas using distribution channels similar to direct broadcast satellite television and through alternative distribution opportunities such as catalog/direct marketing, the Internet and marketing through affinity groups.

Maximize Revenue Through Dual Sources We expect to generate revenue by charging a monthly subscription fee of $9.95 and selling limited advertising time on a number of our channels. XM Radio offers a totally new national radio platform for advertisers that solves many of the implementation problems associated with buying radio advertising nationally on a spot or syndicated basis. Additionally, by using our national platform to aggregate geographically disparate groups through affinity and niche programming, we will provide advertisers an effective way to market products and services to these groups. We believe that sales of advertising time will be a significant source of revenue as our subscriber base grows.

Partner with World Class Companies to Reduce Technology Risk We have contracted with world class technology companies to implement the XM Radio system. We believe that the benefits of having these associations will significantly reduce the risks involved in bringing XM Radio to market. These technology companies include Hughes and Alcatel (satellites); Delphi-Delco, Alpine, Pioneer and SHARP (car and home radios); STMicroelectronics (chipsets); and LCC International (repeater network).
