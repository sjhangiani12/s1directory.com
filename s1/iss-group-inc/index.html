The Fortress on the Rock

Priced March 23, 1998

In 1998 companies were connecting their networks to the internet faster than they could secure them. Firewalls kept some threats out, but few tools could find weak spots inside or spot an attack in progress. Internet Security Systems, based in Atlanta, sold both. Its scanners checked networks for known holes, and its RealSecure product watched traffic for signs of attack. The company went public in March 1998 as ISS Group.

The Original Bet

Christopher Klaus wrote the first version of Internet Scanner while he was a student at Georgia Tech, and he founded ISS in 1994. The bet was that security would become a constant process, not a one-time setup. ISS built a research team that tracked new vulnerabilities and updated its products to find them. Its customers included banks, government agencies, and large corporations. That research team, later called X-Force, became one of the best-known names in security.

After the Offering

ISS priced its offering at $22 a share in March 1998, and its stock rose through the internet boom. The company grew into one of the largest pure security vendors, adding managed security services. IBM acquired ISS in 2006 for about $1.3 billion, and its technology became part of IBM's security business. Its founder had built a security leader from a student project.
