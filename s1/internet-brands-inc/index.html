A Street of Many Shops

Priced November 16, 2007

Internet Brands was a company of many websites. It began as CarsDirect, a site for buying a car online, then bought and built hundreds of focused sites and communities on cars, home improvement, travel, health and law. Each was small, but together they drew a large audience and steady advertising. It went public in November 2007, just before the financial crisis.

The Original Bet

CarsDirect was started at Bill Gross's Idealab in 1998, with Scott Painter, as one of the early bets that people would buy big-ticket items online. When that market proved hard, the company changed course. It began buying established enthusiast sites and forums cheaply, then ran them on shared technology and sold advertising across the network. The model worked because each site already had a loyal, specific audience.

After the Offering

Internet Brands priced its IPO at $8 a share in November 2007 on the Nasdaq. In 2010 Hellman & Friedman bought the company for about $640 million. KKR later acquired it, and in 2017 Internet Brands bought WebMD, another company in this directory. The company kept growing by buying communities and specialist sites, and the WebMD deal made it a major force in online health.
