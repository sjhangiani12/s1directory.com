Asking the Whole World

Priced September 26, 2018

SurveyMonkey made asking questions cheap. Anyone with a browser could build a survey, send a link and watch the answers arrive. Students, small businesses and Fortune 500 companies all used it, and its name became a generic word for an online poll. The company filed as SVMK Inc. in 2018, after years as one of Silicon Valley's best known private companies. It hoped to show that a freemium tool could also sell to large enterprises.

The Original Bet

Ryan Finley started SurveyMonkey in 1999 as a simple tool for building online surveys. His brother Chris joined him, and the company grew for a decade with almost no outside money. Investors bought it in 2009, and Dave Goldberg led it until his death in 2015. The bet was that millions of small paying users could support a large, durable software business.

The Answers Come In

SVMK priced at $12 a share in September 2018 and rose strongly on its first day. The company renamed itself Momentive Global in 2021 to stress its enterprise products. A planned merger with Zendesk fell apart after shareholders objected. In 2023 the private equity firm STG bought the company for about $1.5 billion and took it private. Zander Lurie was chief executive at the time of the offering.
