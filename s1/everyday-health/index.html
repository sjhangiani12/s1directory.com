The Physician at Home

Priced March 28, 2014

Everyday Health ran a network of health websites and apps for consumers and doctors. Readers came for advice on diet, illness and medicine, and pharmaceutical companies paid to reach them. The company also partnered with known names in health media. When it filed to go public in 2014, its earlier attempt in 2011 had been shelved, and health information online was becoming big business.

The Original Bet

Ben Wolin and Mike Keriakos founded the business in 2002 as Waterfront Media. Its early successes included online diet programs. Over time it bought and built sites into a large health network and renamed itself Everyday Health. The bet was that health content and tools could draw a big audience and that drug makers would pay to reach both patients and doctors.

Two Years Public

Everyday Health priced at $14 a share in March 2014. It added more services for doctors and grew revenue, but the stock stayed near its offering price. In 2016 j2 Global, now Ziff Davis, bought the company for about $465 million. Everyday Health became a core part of its digital media group. Its sites continued to publish under the Everyday Health name.
