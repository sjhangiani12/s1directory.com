Out of Buffalo

Priced February 09, 2012

Synacor built websites and services that other companies put their names on. Cable and telecom firms used its software for their customer home pages, email and logins. Synacor made money from ads and search on those pages. When it filed to go public in 2012, it was one of the few tech companies of its size based in Buffalo. It was a quiet business.

The Original Bet

Synacor was founded in Buffalo in 1998. It grew by serving internet providers who wanted their own branded portals without building them. The bet was that these companies would rather partner than build, and that the portals would draw enough users to earn steady ad revenue. Its early customers included regional cable and telecom firms. The company also offered login tools that let subscribers reach online video.

A Long Road

Synacor priced at $5 a share in February 2012. The company later bought Zimbra email software to widen its products. Revenue fell as customers changed their portal strategies. In 2021 Centre Lane Partners bought Synacor and took it private. Its email platform was used by many providers around the world. Under private ownership, Synacor continued to serve internet providers. The deal ended nine years as a public company.
