Freight for the Office

Priced June 26, 2013

CDW sells technology to organizations. A company, school or hospital can buy laptops, servers, software licenses and networking gear from CDW, often with setup and support included. Its account managers act as trusted buyers for customers who lack big IT staff. When CDW filed in 2013, it was returning to the public market after a leveraged buyout in 2007. Customers include businesses of every size.

The Original Bet

Michael Krasny started CDW in 1984 by selling used computers through classified ads from his kitchen. The name stood for Computer Discount Warehouse. He built a catalog and phone sales business that grew quickly in the 1990s. The bet was that businesses wanted a reliable place to buy all their technology, with expert advice and fast delivery. Krasny led the company for many years.

Back on the Board

CDW priced at $17 a share in June 2013. Madison Dearborn and Providence Equity had bought the company in 2007 and loaded it with debt. As a public company CDW paid down that debt and grew through cloud, security and services. It became a steady performer among tech stocks and remains public. Its headquarters later moved to Lincolnshire, Illinois. It has paid a dividend since 2013.
