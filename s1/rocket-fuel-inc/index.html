A Comet Across the Sky

Priced September 19, 2013

Rocket Fuel used software to buy online ads. Its systems looked at many signals about each ad slot and decided in real time whether to bid. Advertisers paid it to run campaigns that hit their goals. When Rocket Fuel filed to go public in 2013, the market for automated ad buying was growing fast, and the company was growing with it.

The Original Bet

George John, Richard Frankel and Abhinav Gupta founded Rocket Fuel in 2008. Several of the founders had worked at Yahoo. They believed artificial intelligence could make ad buying much more efficient than people working by hand. The company's rocket theme ran through its offices and brand. The bet was that better algorithms would win the most valuable ad spending. It grew fast.

Burning Out

Rocket Fuel priced at $29 a share in September 2013 and nearly doubled on its first day. The stock fell over the next years as competition grew and margins shrank. Its purchase of [x+1] in 2014 added debt and complexity. In 2017 Sizmek bought Rocket Fuel for about $145 million. Sizmek itself filed for bankruptcy in 2019. Investors who bought at the offering lost most of their money.
