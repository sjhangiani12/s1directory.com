Aiming at the Buyer

Priced May 16, 2007

TechTarget published websites for people who buy technology at work. Its many sites covered storage, security, networking and other topics in depth, and readers registered to download white papers and research. Technology vendors paid TechTarget to reach those readers and to receive leads on who was researching a purchase. In 2007 trade magazines were losing ground, and TechTarget's online model was taking their place.

The Original Bet

Greg Strakosch and Don Hawk founded TechTarget in 1999. Their bet was that specialized content could attract IT professionals at the moment they were researching purchases, and that vendors would pay well to know who they were. The company built dozens of focused sites, each a target for a narrow group of buyers. Readers got free content, and vendors paid to reach them.

After the Offering

TechTarget priced its IPO at $13 a share in May 2007 on the Nasdaq. It shifted further toward selling data on buying intent. In 2024 it combined with Informa Tech's digital businesses, and the new company, majority owned by Informa, kept the TechTarget name and listing. Its sites still serve IT buyers, and its data on who is researching what remains its main product.
