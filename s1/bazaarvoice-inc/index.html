Voices from the Bazaar

Priced February 23, 2012

Bazaarvoice powered the reviews on other companies' websites. Retailers and brands used its software to collect ratings and comments from customers and display them next to products. Reviews helped shoppers decide and gave companies feedback. When Bazaarvoice filed to go public in 2012, it served many of the biggest retailers and brands. Online shoppers had come to expect reviews on product pages.

The Original Bet

Brett Hurt founded Bazaarvoice in Austin in 2005. He had earlier built an analytics company. He believed that customer voices could sell products better than marketing copy. The bet was that every online store would need reviews and that a shared network could serve them all. Its network let brands share reviews with the retailers that sold their products. That sharing became an important feature.

A Legal Fight

Bazaarvoice priced at $12 a share in February 2012 and rose on its first day. Soon after, it bought PowerReviews, its main rival, and the Justice Department sued. A court ruled against the deal, and Bazaarvoice had to sell PowerReviews. In 2018 Marlin Equity Partners bought the company for about $521 million. It continues to operate as a private company in Austin.
