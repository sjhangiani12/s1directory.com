A Room of One's Own

Priced October 02, 2014

Wayfair sold furniture on the internet. Its site offered millions of products, from sofas to light fixtures, and most of them shipped straight from suppliers. People had long doubted that customers would buy bulky furniture without seeing it. When Wayfair filed to go public in 2014, its sales were growing fast, but it was spending heavily on advertising and losing money.

The Original Bet

Niraj Shah and Steve Conine started the business in 2002 as CSN Stores. They ran hundreds of small websites selling things like racks and bar stools. In 2011 they merged the sites under the Wayfair name. The bet was that a single giant online store for the home could beat furniture showrooms on choice and price, while suppliers handled the stock.

Furnishing the Future

Wayfair priced at $29 a share in October 2014 and rose on its first day. Sales soared during the 2020 pandemic as people improved their homes, then fell back. The company cut staff and opened its first large physical stores. Wayfair remains a public company led by its founders. It also built its own delivery network for large items. Niraj Shah remained chief executive.
