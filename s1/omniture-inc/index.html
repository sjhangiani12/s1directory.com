The Eye That Sees Every Click

Priced June 27, 2006

Omniture showed companies what happened on their websites. Its SiteCatalyst service tracked every page view and click, so marketers could see where visitors came from, what they did and what they bought. It sold to large retailers and media companies by subscription. In 2006 online sales were booming, and measuring them had become a job for serious software. Its customers included many of the largest retailers online.

The Original Bet

Josh James and John Pestana, two young Utah entrepreneurs, founded the company in 1996. Their bet was that businesses would want detailed, real-time data on their online customers, and would pay for it as a hosted service. Omniture won large clients and later added tools for testing and targeting. Its customers paid by volume, so its revenue grew as their websites grew.

After the Offering

Omniture priced its IPO at $6.50 a share in June 2006 on the Nasdaq. It grew quickly and bought several rivals, including WebSideStory and Visual Sciences. In 2009 Adobe bought Omniture for about $1.8 billion, and its software became the core of Adobe's marketing business. Omniture's analytics live on today as Adobe Analytics, part of its Experience Cloud. The deal set Adobe on the road to its large marketing software business.
