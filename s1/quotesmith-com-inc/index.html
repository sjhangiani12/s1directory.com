Shelter, Compared Side by Side

Priced August 03, 1999

Shopping for life or health insurance once meant meeting an agent who showed you a few policies, chosen by them. Quotesmith.com let people compare prices from many insurers on their own, then buy. When it filed to go public in the summer of 1999, consumers were starting to research big purchases online, and insurance looked ripe for the same price comparison that was reshaping travel and books.

The Original Bet

Robert Bland founded the company in 1984, long before the web, as an insurance price comparison service. The bet that came with the internet was that consumers would buy insurance directly once they could see the market for themselves. Quotesmith showed quotes from many insurers and earned commissions as a licensed broker, with its own staff completing the sale. It was early.

After the Offering

Quotesmith priced at $11 a share in August 1999. Its shares fell quickly as investors soured on internet companies, and growth proved slower than hoped. It bought the Insure.com website in 2001 and later renamed itself after it. In 2009 the company sold the Insure.com and Quotesmith names, became Life Quotes, Inc., and went private in 2010. Bland led the company through every stage.
