Games Around the Table

Priced December 15, 2011

Zynga made games for Facebook. Titles such as FarmVille and Zynga Poker drew tens of millions of players who invited their friends to join. Most games were free, and money came from players buying virtual goods. When it filed to go public in 2011, Zynga was the largest social game maker in the world. Social games were a new category.

The Original Bet

Mark Pincus founded Zynga in 2007 and named it after his bulldog. He saw that Facebook's platform could spread games among friends quickly. The bet was that simple, free games played in short sessions would attract a mass audience, and that a small share of players would pay. Zynga Poker was one of its first hits. FarmVille followed in 2009.

After the Bell

Zynga priced at $10 a share in December 2011 and fell below that on its first day. As Facebook changed its rules and players moved to phones, Zynga struggled. It later rebuilt as a mobile game publisher through acquisitions. In 2022 Take-Two Interactive bought Zynga for about $12.7 billion. Its mobile hits included Words With Friends. Take-Two kept Zynga as its mobile games label.
