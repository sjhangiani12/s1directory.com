What's for Dinner

Priced April 04, 2014

Grubhub put restaurant menus online. Diners searched for nearby restaurants, ordered, and paid on the web or an app, and the restaurant handled the delivery at first. Grubhub earned a commission on each order. When it filed to go public in 2014, it had recently merged with Seamless, its New York rival, and it was the leading online food ordering service in the United States.

The Original Bet

Matt Maloney and Mike Evans started Grubhub in Chicago in 2004. Maloney was tired of hunting for paper takeout menus. The bet was that an online directory of menus with easy ordering would become the default way to get takeout. Merging with Seamless in 2013 combined the strongest positions in New York and Chicago. The company kept its headquarters in Chicago.

Delivery Wars

Grubhub priced at $26 a share in April 2014 and rose on its first day. Rivals such as DoorDash and Uber Eats then raced in with their own couriers, and Grubhub lost share. In 2021 Just Eat Takeaway bought the company for about $7.3 billion. Three years later it sold Grubhub to Wonder at a far lower value. Wonder kept the Grubhub name.
