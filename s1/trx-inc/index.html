Behind Every Ticket

Priced September 27, 2005

TRX worked behind the scenes of business travel. It processed bookings and transactions for travel agencies and corporate travel managers, provided reservation and customer support services, and offered data tools that showed companies how their employees traveled. In 2005 online booking was spreading, and travel agencies were outsourcing more of their back-office work. Its customers included large travel agencies and corporations.

The Original Bet

TRX bet that travel agencies and corporations would hand off the routine, high-volume work of processing travel to a specialist. It combined hosted technology with call centers and processing staff. Over time it put more weight on travel data, helping companies track spending and negotiate with airlines and hotels. Its corporate travel data business showed companies where their money went and where they could save.

After the Offering

TRX priced its IPO at $9 a share in September 2005 on the Nasdaq. It spent several years as a small public company while it shifted toward travel data. In 2013 Concur, the travel and expense software maker, acquired TRX. Concur, which SAP bought the following year, folded TRX's travel data tools into its own corporate travel products. Its data tools lived on inside Concur.
