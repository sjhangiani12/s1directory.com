Overview

For the first time in history, intelligence is something humans can build. The cognitive capability to reason, analyze, synthesize, decide, and create is producible at scale and accessible on demand. We believe this abundance of AI will rapidly transform every sector of the global economy in ways greater than the impact of industrialization, electricity, semiconductors, and the internet. AI that truly delivers on its potential—with the risks well managed—could significantly accelerate global growth and dramatically improve quality of life for people around the world.

Anthropic was founded in 2021 by a team of researchers responsible for many of the breakthroughs that define AI. We are first and foremost a research lab—our mission is the responsible development and maintenance of advanced AI for the long-term benefit of humanity. This mission directs our focus—along with our talent and culture, our frontier and safety research, our approach to compute, and our enterprise strategy, it is one of the core advantages that set us apart. These advantages enable us to continuously advance frontier AI and shape the standards for its safe and responsible development. Our frontier AI, Claude, is delivered to customers through our models, platform, and applications. Claude has experienced the fastest enterprise adoption of any technology in history, as demonstrated by revenue growing from $0 at commercial launch in the first quarter of 2023 to $ for the three months ended September 30, 2026. This rapid and growing adoption generates two self-reinforcing dynamics—an improvement loop that advances Claude and an investment loop that helps fund our continued research in frontier AI.

Our Core Advantages

Mission & Focus. All of our efforts are guided by a singular focus on our mission. This focus enables us to concentrate talent, research, and compute investment on the highest-value work for humanity and to avoid conflicting priorities. Achieving our mission means the world gains the enormous benefits of frontier AI while mitigating the most significant risks, and we believe this requires safety and capability to advance together.

Talent & Culture. Our mission and focus enable us to attract and retain world-class talent, including researchers and engineers who want their work to shape a better future. We also have a culture that promotes a low-ego, truth-seeking environment, where frontier AI capability and safety are mutually reinforcing. We believe our culture has driven our talent density as we have scaled.

Frontier & Safety Research. We believe that sustained, focused research at the frontier of intelligence is required to build the most capable and safest AI systems. Our frontier capabilities and safety research are deeply integrated and directly shape model design, training, and deployment, helping us produce systems that are more reliable, steerable, and well-suited for real-world deployment, particularly in demanding enterprise environments. We translate our research leadership into innovation velocity, and have consistently released better models, platform capabilities, and applications. Each version of a Claude model assists in building the next, helping to compress development timelines and produce compounding improvements in the speed at which we innovate. We refer to this recursive dynamic as “closing the loop,” and we believe it is one of the most significant structural advantages in frontier AI.

Our Approach to Compute. We manage compute as a single, fungible pool from multiple strategic partners, and we dynamically direct available compute between workloads based on where the marginal unit is expected to be most productive at any given time. We believe that this approach to compute enables us to deliver superior models, serve more customers, and achieve greater productivity from our compute investment. This compute efficiency creates the foundation for us to advance the frontier of AI capabilities through research and model development, meet growing demand from customers, and provide for internal usage of Claude. We have achieved this through our research breakthroughs and our multi-chip compute architecture across AWS Trainium, Google TPUs, NVIDIA GPUs, and AMD GPUs, enabling us to orchestrate workloads to optimize for price and performance. To support our research, model development, customer demand, and internal usage of Claude, we continue to invest at scale. Enterprise Strategy. Our enterprise strategy is built on the belief that frontier AI is most valuable in real-world settings, as deployment across enterprises is where economic impact is greatest and where the improvement loops from customer feedback are richest. Accordingly, we have organized our research priorities, our product roadmap, and our go-to-market and Applied AI teams around enterprise use cases, which range from routine knowledge work to the most complex problems. The customer feedback we gather directs our research focus and makes Claude more capable over time. This broadens where Claude can be deployed and further expands enterprise adoption, which helps ensure that the benefits of our AI reach people and organizations at scale.

AI is Transforming Intelligence

We believe that Powerful AI, a highly capable and impactful form of frontier AI characterized by the following key attributes, will become widely available within a few years:

•cognitive capabilities matching or exceeding those of top human experts across most professional and scientific disciplines;

•the ability to navigate all interfaces available to a human working virtually, including text, audio, video, and direct computer control;

•the ability to reason through complex tasks autonomously over extended periods—lasting hours, days, or weeks—and seek clarification and feedback when needed;

•the ability to coordinate across multiple instances and collaborate with both humans and other AI systems; and •the ability to interface with the physical world by digitally controlling laboratory equipment, robotic systems, and other machines through digital connections.

If Powerful AI is directed at solving humanity’s hardest problems and the risks are managed responsibly, the potential is extraordinary. The possibilities include compressing decades of scientific progress into years;

accelerating drug discovery, disease detection, and the delivery of personalized therapy; providing individually tailored education and expert financial and legal advice; advancing power grids and battery design for greater efficiency and reliability; and extending to the developing world capabilities that took wealthy nations generations to build.

We believe it will make humans far more capable and expand the world’s overall capacity for that work, including accelerating scientific discovery and helping businesses grow. Frontier AI May Reshape Entire Sectors and Accelerate Novel Discoveries

We believe frontier AI represents a larger opportunity than any prior technology cycle, and Claude is already demonstrating this potential. Claude can now execute complex projects autonomously and turn open-ended tasks into finished, high-quality deliverables. These capabilities enable it to transform the largest sectors of the economy, driving meaningful gains in productivity and output. Frontier AI’s capabilities may extend beyond current markets to open entirely new ones, reaching domains such as robotics, advanced manufacturing, and logistics, and accelerating progress in scientific research, including drug discovery and materials science. For example, in the life sciences sector, where annual global pharmaceutical spend is expected to exceed $2 trillion in 2026,3 Claude is used in drug discovery and development by our customers, our biotechnology partners, and our own researchers. We are also extending Claude into robotics, where our Model Hardware Standard enables AI agents to operate lab and manufacturing equipment such as microscopes, liquid handlers, and off-the-shelf robotic arms. Ultimately, we believe frontier AI will help drive global economic growth. As frontier AI makes workers and organizations more productive, becomes embedded in the products and services they deliver, and unlocks new business models, it creates economic output that did not previously exist. We believe our opportunity is to capture a share not only of today’s economic activity but also of that newly created output as the global economy expands.

The Importance of Safety and Ethical Behavior

While the potential benefits of AI are extraordinary, its rapid advancement will impact the economy and society in ways that could create significant disruption, particularly if the technology is not developed and deployed responsibly. For example, bad actors have already conducted high-profile cyberattacks using AI’s capabilities to discover vulnerabilities, generate malware, and evade detection tools; other risks associated with misuse, including in the domain of biology, could result in even greater harm. AI deployed at scale also has the potential to concentrate power and wealth both within and between countries in ways that could erode trust, harm society, and destabilize the geopolitical order. Misaligned AI systems could corrupt institutional decision- making or cause large-scale harm through autonomous actions that are not visible or understandable to their users and operators. We believe that frontier AI companies must invest in safety and alignment to help ensure the responsible diffusion of this technology. We also believe that safety, alignment, and ethical behavior resonate particularly strongly with enterprises, where rigorous safeguards, predictable model behavior, and demonstrable accountability have become decisive criteria in selecting an AI partner for mission-critical workloads.

Claude as a Virtual Collaborator

We believe we have reached an inflection point where AI systems can function as genuine collaborators in professional work. They quickly complete substantive tasks that might otherwise occupy a skilled professional for several hours, a full day, or longer. AI systems are also becoming increasingly capable of performing tasks that were previously too difficult or costly to complete or surfacing original insights and analyses. We refer to these AI systems that work alongside humans to perform complex, multi-step work at professional levels as Virtual Collaborators. Virtual Collaborators carry the access rights, system connections, and context that their organizations grant them, which allows them to function more accurately and reliably. They can navigate and use internal tools and execute workflows that span multiple steps that require sustained reasoning. Collaborators are AI designed to be embedded where work gets done, and our aim is to continue extending them to knowledge workers globally, a population that is estimated to exceed one billion in 2028.4 Claude Code. The clearest manifestation of Virtual Collaborators today is Claude Code, our coding agent and the product we believe has most concretely defined what autonomous AI work looks like. Claude Code shifted the market’s understanding of what an AI system could do in collaboration with software developers: the model suggests the next line of code and goes further by planning, writing, testing, and debugging—a process that results in production-grade code. Within months of its release, autonomous coding became the reference point for measuring the progress of frontier AI. For enterprise AI use cases, we believe the launch of Claude Code was comparable in its impact to the introduction of the first widely adopted conversational AI assistants. What makes Claude Code a Virtual Collaborator is its sustained reasoning, a harness that translates intent into actions, an execution environment that maintains a persistent state whether on a developer’s machine or in a sandboxed cloud environment, integration with existing tools and systems, and a user-friendly interface. Its uses are highly varied and often surprising: for example, NASA engineers used Claude Code to plan and write navigation commands for the Mars Perseverance rover, resulting in the first AI-planned drive on Mars.

Software developers are powerful advocates for Claude—they understand how systems work, have credibility evaluating technical tools, and often serve as internal champions for adopting new technologies. Winning their trust creates a foundation for broader diffusion of Virtual Collaborators across enterprise workforces. We are already seeing this pattern play out with non-technical teams increasingly adopting Claude Code and Claude Cowork to manage their own workflows.

Claude Cowork. All digital work is ultimately an expression of code, and Claude’s mastery of it powers Virtual Collaborators capable of handling complex work far beyond software development. Claude Cowork extends the same agentic architecture of Claude Code to knowledge workers broadly by translating a user’s intent into completed work, autonomously reading files, navigating applications, and executing multi-step workflows on a user’s desktop while the underlying code is abstracted away. By extending beyond Claude Code with applications purpose-built for specific professions and industry verticals, we accelerate Claude’s diffusion into new categories of knowledge work and deepen our presence across the enterprise. As of September 30, 2026, adoption of Claude Cowork, which we made available as a research preview in January 2026, outpaced the adoption Claude Code achieved at the equivalent point following its research preview release in February 2025.

In September 2026, we began merging Claude Cowork and Claude.ai, bringing agentic and conversational work into a unified application. Users can now move from a simple question to complex, multi-step tasks in the same conversation.

Extending Virtual Collaborators. We believe the trajectory for Virtual Collaborators is clear and accelerating.

Each generation of Claude models has expanded along multiple dimensions: the depth and rigor of their reasoning, the duration and autonomy of the work they can sustain, the complexity of tasks they can complete, and the volume of useful output they can produce. Capability evaluations and benchmarking have shown substantial improvements across Claude model versions in the duration and complexity of tasks that can be completed reliably, with more recent model versions demonstrating particularly significant advances.

We are building towards a world where Claude can be given open-ended tasks lasting hours, days, or weeks, and then execute them autonomously with the judgment and initiative of a brilliant colleague. Ultimately, millions of Virtual Collaborators can run in parallel, each working independently on unrelated problems or collaborating seamlessly to achieve a shared objective. The result is something without historical precedent:

what we refer to as a country of geniuses in a data center, available to every organization and sector of the economy.

Enterprise Adoption of Claude

The speed and depth of our enterprise customer adoption are powerful evidence of Claude’s position at the frontier of AI. Our customer base as of September 30, 2026 includes:

•More than 11,900 customers with $100,000 or more of revenue on a TTM basis;

•More than 1,900 customers with $1 million or more of revenue on a TTM basis; and •Approximately 250 customers with $10 million or more of revenue on a TTM basis.

We believe customers choose Claude for a wide variety of use cases, from routine knowledge work to their most important problems. They come to us for access to frontier AI across our platform and applications that deliver real-world outcomes. We see this conviction in customer behavior: enterprises consistently utilize our latest and most capable models as soon as they are released, gravitating to the most advanced option for their highest- value work. Our customers see us as a long-term partner for the frontier capabilities we offer today, for the innovation they expect from us in the future, and for our ability to meet their needs across the capability-cost- speed curve.

Beyond our models, the Claude Platform provides primitives that enable customers to accelerate time-to-value and leverage Claude’s intelligence in a variety of use cases. These primitives are the same ones we use to build our own applications. Our applications, such as Claude Code and Claude Cowork, multiply the impact of employees. Over time, our applications have become more capable and are now frequently being used to conduct long-running autonomous work alongside employees. Improvements to our models, platform, and applications accrue to the benefit of our customers and drive continued adoption.

Our commitment to our mission instills confidence in our customers to choose Claude as a trusted partner. Our investments in research on AI alignment, interpretability, cybersecurity, and enterprise-grade controls aim to deliver the predictability and dependability that organizations need in customer-facing and regulated contexts.

This trust means that enterprises can expand the scope of the work that they delegate to Claude, as well as the systems into which Claude is integrated. Enterprises are aligning their strategies around Claude with the confidence that we are building frontier AI that serves their needs in the long term.

Today, enterprises rely on Claude to reason, plan, and execute across complex workflows with a level of trustworthiness and judgment that we believe was not previously available from earlier generations of AI.

Customers realize value from Claude in three primary ways: more productive employees, faster processes, and embedded intelligence in their products. As Claude’s capabilities grow, our customers benefit and often broaden their adoption of Claude in new workflows. This expanding role reflects a broader shift in how Claude is used inside the enterprise, from a tool that responds to discrete prompts to a Virtual Collaborator.

We drive the diffusion of Claude primarily by making it accessible across multiple product surfaces. For example:

•Claude Platform, our comprehensive agentic development environment, provides customers with direct access to the primitives to build, deploy, and scale their own applications that leverage Claude’s capabilities within their own products and workflows.

•Claude Code, our coding agent, works alongside human software developers on end-to-end software development: writing, planning, editing, testing, debugging, and committing code across production- scale codebases.

•Claude Cowork, our knowledge work agent, has computer use capability that enables it to work with professionals on end-to-end business tasks: autonomously researching, drafting, analyzing, synthesizing, and executing other knowledge work.
